Humidifier Bottle — Product Overview

The CPX Flowmeter Humidifier Bottle is a medical device used to add moisture to dry oxygen before it reaches the patient during oxygen therapy. Dry medical oxygen can irritate the nasal passages, throat, and airways; humidification improves patient comfort and tolerance during extended therapy sessions.

Manufactured from durable polycarbonate, this humidifier bottle is shatter-proof, easy to clean, and can be sterilised in an autoclave at 121°C for up to 40 cycles. It connects directly to a medical gas flowmeter using a moving pipe union, making installation quick and secure. As part of our medical gas shop, this component is stocked for immediate delivery across Kenya.
