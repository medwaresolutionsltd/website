Medical Gas Systems: The Importance of Copper Pipes

Why medical-grade copper pipes are the backbone of any safe hospital medical gas system.

Medware Editorial Team Experts in Healthcare Engineering

The Backbone of Hospital Infrastructure

When installing a medical gas pipeline system, the quality of the materials is non-negotiable. Medical grade, degreased copper pipes are the global standard for delivering oxygen, medical air, and vacuum.

We source top-tier equipment from CPx and other reputable manufacturers. Every installation strictly adheres to ISO 7396-1 standards to prevent contamination and ensure structural integrity.

Installation Best Practices

Brazing copper pipes requires specialized inert gas shielding to prevent internal oxidation. Our engineers are certified to perform these critical joints, ensuring patient safety at every terminal unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why must medical copper pipes be degreased?

Any residual oil or grease inside the pipe can react violently with pressurized oxygen, posing a severe fire hazard.

What standards govern medical gas pipelines?

ISO 7396-1 and HTM 02-01 are the leading international standards for medical gas pipeline systems.
